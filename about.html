About Guidestone Solutions

A dedicated team providing clear direction.

Guidestone Solutions is led by a dedicated team that works directly with clients to develop strategy, shape messages, and move important work forward.

Our story

Built for thoughtful, hands-on consulting.

Guidestone Solutions is a consulting firm currently focused on local political campaigns, including school boards, county commissions, city offices, sheriff campaigns, and other county elected positions.

We believe it matters who governs and that local government is closest to the people it serves. These campaigns have a direct effect on communities, public services, schools, public safety, and local priorities.

The Guidestone Team created the firm to give clients direct access to the people developing the strategy, shaping the message, and carrying out the work.

As Guidestone Solutions grows, the firm plans to apply the same hands-on approach to businesses, nonprofit organizations, associations, community groups, and other clients seeking strategic guidance, message development, and effective communications.
